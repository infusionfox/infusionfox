{# Cumulative dose reference (replaces the old "Daily ceiling" section) #}
Cumulative dose reference

No validated maximum daily dose exists in veterinary patients. The tier flags above are checkpoints, not stop signs.

  • Within conservative guideline: ≤ {{ "%.0f"|format(result.cumulative_guideline_ml_per_kg) }} mL/kg (Fernandez 2011)
  • Above conservative guideline: {{ "%.0f"|format(result.cumulative_guideline_ml_per_kg) }}–{{ "%.0f"|format(result.cumulative_high_ml_per_kg) }} mL/kg, routinely seen in practice, particularly the slow protocol
  • High cumulative: > {{ "%.0f"|format(result.cumulative_high_ml_per_kg) }} mL/kg, approaches human ASRA upper bound; check lipemia and fat-overload markers

Fat overload syndrome is rate-driven (peak plasma triglyceride) rather than total-dose-driven, which is why the slow protocol tolerates a higher cumulative volume at ~25% the peak triglyceride load of the fast protocol.

{# Clinical stopping criteria (moved up; was inside Monitoring) #}
Clinical stopping criteria
  • Clinical response: improvement of the targeted toxicity signs (mental status, cardiovascular function, tremor activity)
  • Lipemia status: visible lipemia before any repeat dose suggests reduced clearance; defer the next dose or extend the interval
  • Fat-overload signs: rising triglycerides, hemolysis on CBC, transaminitis, jaundice, or coagulopathy
  • Lack of response after 12–24 hours of therapy: discontinue; further ILE is unlikely to help

{% endif %} {# Monitoring + post-protocol guidance #}
Monitoring
  • Continuous ECG and BP during and for at least 30 min after the infusion
  • Repeat physical exam every 5–10 min during the active protocol
  • CBC, chemistry, and triglycerides 4–6 hours after the protocol; assess for fat overload (hyperlipidemia, hemolysis, transaminitis)
  • Pulse oximetry can be unreliable for hours after ILE because of lipemia interference at the sensor
  • For ongoing toxicity beyond the protocol: pair with supportive care (vasopressors, antiarrhythmics, decontamination, intubation if needed) and consult toxicology where available
