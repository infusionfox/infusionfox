Clinical background — at a glance

Tube selection

  • NG / NE (3.5–8 Fr): liquid diets only, <5 days.
  • E-tube (12–14 Fr): blenderized canned OK, >5 days or anesthetized for another reason.

When to start

  • Within 24–48 hr if not eating voluntarily.
  • Triggers: anorexia >3 days, intake <80% RER × 3 days, >10% weight loss, BCS ≤3/9.
  • Cats + obesity + any anorexia: start sooner (lipidosis).

When to discontinue

  • Eating ~75% RER × 2–3 days, stable weight.
  • Taper over 2–3 days, do not stop abruptly.
  • No food by mouth × 3–5 days to reduce aversion (esp. cats).
